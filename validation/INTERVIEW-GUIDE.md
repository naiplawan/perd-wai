# Perd Wai — Phase 0 Interview Guide

## Objective

Learn whether a first-time Thai café founder can use a cash-flow forecast to make a concrete launch decision.

Do not sell the product or validate the feature list. Validate the problem, the user's mental model, and the usefulness of the decision output.

## Participant mix

- 3 people actively considering opening a café or drink shop within the next 24 months.
- 2 Thai accountants, café operators, or small-business mentors.

Record participant type, relevant experience, and whether they have already made a budget. Do not record unnecessary personal or financial details.

## Session structure — 30 minutes

### 1. Current behavior — 8 minutes

Ask:

- What are you trying to decide about opening the business?
- How have you estimated the money required so far?
- Which number are you least confident about?
- What would make you delay, change, or cancel the plan?

Capture the participant's own words. Do not introduce “runway,” “gross margin,” or “break-even” until they use the terms or see the prototype.

### 2. Prototype task — 12 minutes

Give this instruction:

> Imagine you are deciding whether to open this café. Enter your best current assumptions, review the forecast, then change one assumption to test a less favorable case. Tell me what you would do next.

Do not coach. Record:

- Where they hesitate or abandon a field.
- Which assumptions they cannot provide.
- Whether they notice the cash-runway result.
- Whether they can explain why the result changed.
- The next action they name.

### 3. Comprehension check — 5 minutes

Ask:

- In your own words, what does “cash runs out in month 3” mean?
- Which assumption has the biggest effect on your result?
- What is the difference between profit and cash in this forecast?
- Which number would you verify before making a real decision?

### 4. Closing — 5 minutes

Ask:

- Did this change your launch decision or next action?
- What felt confusing or untrustworthy?
- What would you expect the tool to show before using it for a real business?

## Evidence rubric

Score each founder participant as pass/fail for each criterion:

| Criterion | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Completion | Completes the scenario without facilitator coaching |
| Understanding | Correctly explains cash runway in their own words |
| Scenario reasoning | Explains why changing an assumption changes the outcome |
| Decision | Names a concrete next action or changes the launch decision |
| Trust | Identifies at least one number they would verify before relying on the result |

Phase 0 passes only if the thresholds in `PLAN.md` are met. A feature request is not evidence of success unless it explains a failed task or comprehension point.
