# Perd Wai — Prototype Test Checklist

## Critical task

The participant must:

1. Choose the café/drink-shop scenario.
2. Enter or confirm the required assumptions.
3. Reach the six-month cash-flow forecast.
4. Identify the month cash becomes negative or confirm that it does not within the forecast.
5. Change one assumption to create a pessimistic scenario.
6. Explain the result and state a concrete next action.

## Success measures

| Measure | Collection method | Phase 0 target |
|---|---|---:|
| Unassisted completion | Observer marks coaching events | 4 of 5 participants |
| Cash-runway comprehension | Participant explains result without definition supplied | 4 of 5 participants |
| Scenario comprehension | Participant explains the effect of one changed assumption | 4 of 5 participants |
| Concrete decision | Participant names a next action or changes launch decision | 4 of 5 founders; 3 of 5 in Phase 4 |
| Model credibility | Participant names a number they would verify | 4 of 5 participants |

## Observer rules

- Count a request for clarification as a friction event; do not immediately solve it.
- Distinguish a missing input from a confusing label.
- Record the exact screen, field, or result that caused the problem.
- Do not treat positive comments as validation unless the participant completes the task.
- Stop and note a safety issue if the participant interprets the output as a guarantee or tax advice.
